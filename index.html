Lease essentials

Lease terms, explained.

A short glossary to help both sides negotiate with confidence. This is general information, not legal or tax advice.

Lock-in period

A period during which neither side can exit without penalty. Longer lock-ins can help a tenant negotiate a better rent.

Rent escalation

A planned rent increase, often applied yearly or at a set interval. Test different rates in the lease calculator above.

Security deposit

A refundable amount held by the landlord, usually expressed as a number of months of rent.

Rent-free fit-out period

Time given to build interiors before rent starts. It is often negotiable, especially on longer leases.

Maintenance (CAM)

Common area maintenance for shared services such as lifts, security and power backup. In many buildings it is billed on top of rent.

GST, stamp duty and registration

Commercial rent may attract GST, and lease agreements are subject to stamp duty and registration rules that vary by state. Confirm with your legal or tax advisor.

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

What is LeaseProp?

LeaseProp is a new, technology-led leasing company based in Noida. It works between landlords and tenants, helping businesses find the right office space and helping property owners connect with genuine corporate tenants.

Who do you work with?

Startups and established businesses looking for office space, and property owners, developers and workspace operators with space to lease.

Which areas do you cover?

We start in Noida and Greater Noida and also work across the wider NCR, including Gurugram and New Delhi.

I am a landlord with vacant space. What happens next?

Send us the location, size, expected rent and availability through the form. We will review the property, discuss positioning and pricing, and introduce suitable tenants.

Can startups lease small spaces or co-working seats?

Yes. We cover everything from co-working seats and managed offices to full office floors, so a small team can start light and expand later.

How long does it take to close a lease?

It depends on the property, the requirement and the negotiation. A clear brief and lease-ready documents on the landlord's side usually speed things up.

Are the rates on the website final?

No. Listing rates are indicative and the lease calculator gives an estimate. Final terms are confirmed on enquiry and set in the lease agreement.

Do you provide legal or tax advice?

No. We coordinate the leasing process, but we recommend that both parties take legal and tax advice on the agreement.
